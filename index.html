Maker's Lab

Emlyon's Makers'Lab is a space for creativity and experimentation where students can turn ideas into tangible projects. It offers tools and equipment to design, build and test prototypes while exploring new techniques.

Designed for collaboration, the space encourages hands-on learning and the sharing of skills. Students can work independently or in teams, exchange ideas with fellow makers, and bring creative, entrepreneurial or technology projects to life.
